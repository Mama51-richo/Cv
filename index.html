CV Tips

1. Keep it relevant.
Focus on experience and skills related to the position.

2. Use measurable achievements.
Where possible, show results with numbers, percentages or time saved.

3. Keep formatting consistent.
Use clear headings, short bullets and consistent dates.

4. Check before sending.
Review spelling, contact details and dates before exporting.

5. Use a professional email.
A simple name-based email is usually preferable.
